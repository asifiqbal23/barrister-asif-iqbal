School / juristic method

A school is a way of interpreting inheritance rules. Choose a known method, or keep “Compare all methods” to see the six alternatives.

02

Money & debts

Use one currency throughout. Only include the deceased’s own property. All three debt fields are added once; disputed priority or insolvency requires review.

Enter only land already available to the heirs after debts, expenses and bequests. Cash deductions cannot be converted into land without a valuation; do not deduct them twice.

04Special circumstances+

Select relevant circumstances. Unborn-child and uncertain-eligibility inputs produce alternative illustrations; other complex cases stop for review.

Do not enter an adopted child or foster relative as a blood relative solely because of adoption or fosterage. Enter any independently established blood relationship separately.

Include these people in the surviving-relative counts in section 07. The results compare their inclusion and exclusion; the calculator does not decide eligibility.

05Reversion of surplus (radd)+

An override is a user-selected assumption, not a statement that every school adopts it. Unallocated property is shown separately for review, including any possible Bayt al-Mal treatment.

06

Will / bequest

After costs and debts

A bequest is a gift directed by a will. Enter a fraction such as 1/3; it is taken after costs and debts. If the gift is for an heir, select that in Special circumstances.

07

Surviving relatives

Count relatives who were alive at the time of death. Use + and − or type a number. Father means the deceased’s father; son means the deceased’s son.

What do the relationship names mean?

Full brother or sister: the same father and mother. Paternal half-sibling: the same father, different mother. Maternal half-sibling: the same mother, different father. All are relationships to the person who died.

Connecting family branches / আত্মীয়তার শাখা

For Hanbali Tanzil, people in the same category can inherit through different deceased relatives. Enter one branch ID per person, separated by commas. Example: D1,D1,D2 means the first two are children of one daughter; the third is a child of another daughter. For two connecting generations use D1/P1. Use the same ID across fields when the connecting person is the same. Missing necessary branches stop the calculation.

Calculation methods, sources & limitations

This independent educational version covers prescribed shares, common blocking rules, residuaries, awl, configurable radd, Umariyyah, Mushtarakah and specified grandfather–sibling cases including Akdariyyah. It offers 67 relative categories for recording the family.

This is not an implementation of every case. Selected distant-kindred rules are implemented with explicit branch information where needed. Unborn-child and uncertain-eligibility outcomes are conditional. Multiple relationships, successive estates, Bangladesh statutory representation and bequests to heirs require individual review. English and Bengali interfaces are provided; detailed rule explanations remain in English.

For Bangladesh, review section 4 of the Muslim Family Laws Ordinance, 1961 where a child of the deceased died earlier leaving children. This calculator does not apply that statutory adjustment automatically.
