The default Bangladesh profile combines the stated Hanafi model with the statutory treatment below. The separate classical profile is a juristic comparison, not a Bangladesh legal determination. Research checked on 18 October 2026.

What section 4 changes / ধারা ৪-এর পরিবর্তন

When a son or daughter died before the estate owner but left children alive when succession opened, the model represents that predeceased child once. It first calculates the share that child would have received, then assigns that total to the child’s branch. Different branches remain separate even when they contain different numbers of grandchildren. The other relatives’ shares are calculated against the same notional family.

আগে মৃত ছেলে বা মেয়ের প্রতিটি শাখা আলাদা। আগে মৃত সন্তানের অংশ তাঁর জীবিত সন্তানদের শাখায় যায়। আগে মৃত ব্যক্তির স্বামী/স্ত্রীকে এই শাখায় যোগ করা হয় না। সেই সন্তান মূল ব্যক্তির মৃত্যুর পরে মারা গেলে সেটি আলাদা, ধারাবাহিক উত্তরাধিকার; এই ধারা ৪ ইনপুট নয়।

MFLO 1961, section 4 ↗ · Sections 1 and 3: application and statutory priority ↗

One living child in a branch: the branch total is also that child’s share. Several living children: only the collective branch award is calculated. No per-person amount, equal division or 2:1 division is silently inferred. The calculator does not present an unresolved interpretation as a binding Bangladesh rule. The chart, land results, printout and CSV all preserve this distinction.

এক শাখায় একাধিক সন্তান থাকলে চার্টে দেখানো টাকা বা জমি তাঁদের সম্মিলিত অংশ। প্রত্যেকের ভাগ নয়। ব্যক্তিগত বণ্টনের প্রশ্নটি আলাদা পর্যালোচনার জন্য রাখা হয়েছে।

Section 4 identifies the branch entitlement. The reviewed Bangladesh judgment below addresses the timing of succession; it does not establish a universal intrabranch division formula. Academic discussion also records divergent interpretations: A.W.M. Abdul Huq, “Section 4 …: A Critic”, Northern University Journal of Law 1 (2010), pp. 7–13. Academic commentary and foreign cases discussed there are not represented as binding Bangladesh judgments.

When the statutory calculation runs

  • Bangladesh Muslim succession and the stated Hanafi base are selected.
  • The estate owner died after 15 July 1961. Earlier, boundary-date or uncertain cases stop for historical-law review.
  • Each represented parent was a direct son or daughter who died earlier, leaving at least one child alive at the estate owner’s death.
  • Each grandchild is entered in one branch only, with no duplicate ordinary-relative entry.
  • More remote representation, competing relationships, uncertain eligibility, successive estates and bequests to heirs are not resolved by this overlay.

Abdul Ali and another v Manikjan Bibi and others, HCD, Civil Revision 2652 of 2004, judgment 16 August 2017, pp. 7–10: the date on which succession opens matters to the applicability of section 4. This source is cited for that point, not as approval of this software.

Statutes and their practical effect

AuthorityWhat the calculator does
Muslim Personal Law (Shariat) Application Act 1937, s.2Identifies the personal-law basis and its scope. The provision expressly excepts agricultural-land questions. The calculator does not treat that wording as proof of a separate mathematical agricultural share formula; land title, classification and applicable law must be checked.
MFLO 1961, ss.3–4Applies the statutory branch override in the supported cases, with exact fractions and separate branch explanations.
MFLO 1961, ss.7–8A disputed divorce or marital status stops the Bangladesh calculation. Section 7 links effectiveness to the notice period and, where applicable, pregnancy; section 8 applies the relevant provisions to other specified dissolution cases. The tool does not remove a spouse just because talaq was pronounced.
MFLO 1961, s.10Displays the statutory dower source. An established unpaid dower liability entered in the form is deducted before heirs’ shares. Where payment terms are absent, s.10 addresses payment on demand. The amount, validity and enforceability are not guessed. A receivable belonging to a deceased wife is an asset to consider in her estate, not a debt to subtract from it.
Evidence Act 1872, s.108A missing-person flag stops allocation in Bangladesh mode. The statutory burden-of-proof rule is not converted into an invented death date or an automatic heir exclusion.
Provident Funds Act 1925, ss.3–5The special-property question stops automatic pooling where rights are uncertain. Qualifying fund, dependant and nominee rights need the fund rules and documents. This rule is not generalized to every bank account, insurance policy or pension.
Succession Act 1925, s.29 and s.58Does not import Part V’s general intestacy fractions into Muslim succession or treat Part VI’s general testamentary rules as the Muslim will formula. The existing one-third bequest model is a stated personal-law assumption; heir-benefiting wills and disputed consent require review.
State Acquisition and Tenancy Act 1950, s.143BLinks the land result to the inherited-property partition provision. Numerical land shares do not effect a registered partition, mutation, title determination or physical demarcation.

Qur’an, Sunnah and statutory sources

Result explanations retain Qur’an 4:11, 4:12, 4:176, the cited hadith and juristic sources. Statutory citations are displayed alongside them where they affect the model. A statutory branch entitlement is labelled as statutory; it is not attributed to a verse as though the wording were identical.

কুরআন-সুন্নাহ ও ফিকহি সূত্রের পাশাপাশি সংশ্লিষ্ট বাংলাদেশি আইন থাকবে। বিধিবদ্ধ সমন্বয়কে আলাদা করে চিহ্নিত করা হয়।

Calculation coverage / হিসাবের পরিধি

Calculated: the stated Hanafi shares and, in supported section 4 cases, each predeceased child’s collective branch share. If a branch has one living child, that child’s individual share is also shown.

যা গণনা হয়: নির্ধারিত হানাফি মডেলের অংশ এবং প্রযোজ্য ক্ষেত্রে ধারা ৪ অনুযায়ী আগে মৃত প্রতিটি সন্তানের শাখার মোট অংশ। শাখায় একজন জীবিত সন্তান থাকলে তাঁর ব্যক্তিগত অংশও দেখানো হয়।

Not yet calculated: individual shares within a branch containing several children, great-grandchild representation, overlapping relationships and successive estates.

যা এখনও গণনা হয় না: একই শাখার একাধিক সন্তানের পৃথক ভাগ, প্রপৌত্র/প্রপৌত্রীর প্রতিনিধিত্ব, একই ব্যক্তির একাধিক উত্তরাধিকার-সম্পর্ক এবং পরপর একাধিক মৃত্যুর ফলে সৃষ্ট পৃথক সম্পত্তির বণ্টন।

Cases needing documents or further legal review / যে বিষয়ে নথি বা আরও আইনি যাচাই প্রয়োজন

Historical estates, foreign-law conflicts, disputed marriages or parentage, adoption, homicide, unborn heirs, insolvency, contested wills, special benefits and ownership disputes need additional facts, legal review or functionality beyond this Bangladesh model. A reference to an authority does not mean every issue under it is automatically resolved.
