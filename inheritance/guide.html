Start with the family facts.

  1. Choose a school or juristic profile. “No school / compare all” displays six named alternatives.
  2. Enter the monetary estate, funeral/burial expense, unpaid dower and debts. Count each debt only once. Leave the amount at zero for fractions only.
  3. If relevant, open Land & area units and enter land already net of obligations and bequests.
  4. Check Special circumstances, then the reversion (radd) policy if an override is needed. Enter any bequest fraction, up to one-third in this model.
  5. Enter the surviving relatives in the listed groups, including relatives who may be excluded by others. Count every person once. Search by English or Bengali name; provide connecting branches when requested.
  6. Select Calculate shares. Read the result, then open the calculation steps or an individual relative’s explanation and sources. Print or download the selected result if needed.

Learning examples are available below. They do not populate or replace your own family information.

What the result represents.

The calculator applies selected opinions within six profiles: Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi‘i, Hanbali, Imam Abu Yusuf and Imam Muhammad. A school name does not imply that every opinion within that school has been implemented.

AreaImplemented treatment
Heirs and exclusions67 relationship categories: prescribed sharers, descendants, siblings, listed agnates and distant kindred. Excluded relatives remain visible with a reason.
Awl and raddAwl reduces shares proportionately when they exceed the whole. Automatic radd excludes spouses in the Hanafi-family and Hanbali profiles. Classical Maliki/Shafi‘i policy leaves a separately identified balance. Optional overrides are labelled assumptions.
Grandfather and siblingsAbu Hanifa’s profile excludes siblings with the eligible paternal grandfather. The other five profiles use Zayd’s selected approach, including mu‘addah and Akdariyyah.
Distant kindredThe selected Hanafi-family priority and distribution methods, or Hanbali Tanzil with explicit family branches. Under the selected Tanzil view, equally related male/female branch members share equally. Branch IDs identify connecting relatives; they are not personal names.
Unborn or uncertain eligibilityAlternative illustrations, with assumptions shown. These do not establish live birth, survival, parentage, religion or killing-related disqualification, and do not prescribe a legally sufficient reserve.
MoneyExact fractions; two-decimal money reconciled by largest remainders. Rounding can give otherwise equal heirs a one-minor-unit difference, with that adjustment identified.
LandExact unit factors underpin approximate decimal displays. The default marla is 272.25 square feet and kanal 5,445 square feet. Use a custom unit where local conventions differ.

Individual review remains necessary for mixed remote maternal ascendants with sibling descendants or aunts/uncles, Bangladesh section 4 representation, multiple relationships for one person, successive estates or uncertain death order, bequests to heirs and facts outside the listed family model. These selections stop the automatic allocation and explain why. In Bangladesh, section 4 can give children of a predeceased son or daughter a branch share; this calculator does not silently apply that statutory adjustment.

No scholar, court or chamber has certified this tool. The current release does not automate every possible case or reproduce every feature and language of the reference website. See the technical calculation record for the full coverage and testing notes.

Common questions.

Does it send my family or financial data anywhere?

No. The calculator processes inputs locally in your browser and does not save them to a server. Source links take you to their publishers only if you open them. Keep downloaded reports securely.

Why does a relative receive zero?

A nearer eligible heir can exclude a more remote relative, prescribed shares can exhaust the estate, or the selected juristic policy can give that category no allocation. The result explains the relevant reason. Zero does not imply that the family relationship is unimportant.

Why are some amounts one paisa or cent apart?

Exact fractions often produce repeating decimals. The displayed monetary allocations are reconciled to the entered estate. Any one-minor-unit rounding difference is shown separately from the exact entitlement fraction.

How do I enter connecting branches?

For three daughter’s descendants, D1,D1,D2 means two descend from one daughter and the third from another. Use the same connecting ID across relationship fields when they share that parent. For two connecting generations, use a path such as D1/P1. Review the family tree if uncertain.

Can I use it as a final distribution certificate?

No. Confirm ownership, all surviving relatives, debts, wills, eligibility and applicable law before distribution. This is an educational aid and does not create an advocate–client relationship.

Can I update writings or videos on the portfolio?

Yes. The portfolio remains independently hosted on GitHub Pages. Its writing and video entries are maintained in the repository’s profile data; the calculator is a separate section.
