# Portfolio editorial direction

This is Asif Iqbal’s professional brand and portfolio. Its audiences include prospective clients, recruiters, legal employers, referring counsel, and professional collaborators. Help them assess his experience, judgment, communication and contribution through specific, verifiable material.

Preserve the restrained green-and-ivory visual style. Write confidently and precisely; show the scale and substance of work without exaggerating his role. Keep ongoing engagements anonymous, and distinguish proposed transactions from completed ones. Respect the chamber’s engagement arrangements and the existing professional disclaimer; contact language should support professional connections rather than solicit legal instructions.

## Adding writing and video

Maintain the `writing` and `videos` arrays in `profile.json`. Verify each item’s title, authorship, subject and publication date from its source. Use a short original description that explains its relevance. Do not invent content, awards, audiences, view counts or results. Place the newest featured addition first.

For YouTube videos, add `youtubeId` (the 11-character video ID), `url`, `title`, `description`, `category`, and the verified `date`. Use `format: "short"` for portrait Shorts. The site renders a thumbnail and a player activated by the visitor, with a permanent YouTube link. Switching away from the Videos tab stops playback. The `#videos` link opens the Videos tab directly. Other video URLs retain an external watch link.

Keep the viewer’s next step clear: explore professional experience, read the CV, or connect through the existing professional contact section. Avoid internal implementation language in public copy. Check desktop and mobile presentation when changing card layouts. Bump the profile, script and stylesheet version parameters when publishing changes to those files so returning visitors receive the update.

## Public calculator

The calculator is also part of the professional portfolio. Design for a first-time visitor, including a 15-year-old: plain English and Bangla, visible relationship definitions, touch-friendly controls, and exact per-person shares alongside a pie chart. Preserve the established order and all case options; keep technical comparisons and full breakdowns available on demand. Never populate a visitor’s family from a worked example without their action. Charts must include any unallocated balance, omit zero-share slices, distinguish group totals from individual shares, and update with the selected scenario. Preserve the calculation engine and all existing sources and review boundaries when making presentation changes.
